# AIVES: AI-Powered Viva Examination System

Oral examinations—such as capstone defenses, final viva voce assessments, and competency interviews—provide authentic evaluation but face critical operational bottlenecks:

* **High Faculty Overhead:** Consumes disproportionate instructor hours to assess each candidate individually.
* **Lack of Standardization:** Divergent question difficulty and subjective scoring across different examination panels.
* **Scalability Bottlenecks:** Difficult to conduct consistently across large student cohorts.
* **Audit & Transparency Gaps:** Absence of objective artifacts (full verbatim transcripts, itemized rubric scoring) when handling grade appeals.

**AIVES** transforms oral assessments by streamlining the examination lifecycle from question synthesis to verifiable grading:

* **Dynamic Question Synthesis:** Generates domain-specific, curriculum-aligned questions automatically.
* **Adaptive AI Probing:** Conducts interactive oral interviews with context-aware follow-up questions tailored dynamically to student responses.
* **Rubric-Aligned Scoring:** Delivers transparent, criteria-based evaluation to assist examiners in minimizing grading bias.
* **End-to-End Session Audit Trails:** Automatically logs full transcripts, audio records, and itemized score breakdowns for reproducible, dispute-free grading integrity.
